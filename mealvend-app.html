MealVend app

The student app for the vending machines. Tap through it on the phone.

  1. Tap the student card to sign in.
  2. Check the AI prediction and the deals.
  3. Confirm the meal or build your own.
  4. Choose the campus.
  5. Show the pass and simulate the pick-up.

All students, orders and stock in this demo are simulated.
